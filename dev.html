Dev work

Understand the need fast. Build it with AI.

What matters most in development is not flashy technique. It is quickly understanding what you actually need and building exactly that. AI is what makes this possible: I work with it at every step, from shaping the idea to launching and running the site.

How I build

01

Needs before technique

Start from what users and the team must be able to do, not from features that only look impressive.

02

Fast judgement

Turn a first conversation into a clear scope and a working first version quickly.

03

AI at every step

Planning, design, code, content and operations all run with AI, so a small team can launch a complete, working site.
